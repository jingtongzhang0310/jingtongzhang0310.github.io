Welcome!

I'm Jingtong Zhang, an undergraduate student in the Department of Civil and Architectural Engineering at Qingdao University of Technology.

My interests lie in artificial intelligence and deep learning, especially their applications in the AEC (Architecture, Engineering, and Construction) industry. I'm currently focused on designing:

  • Automated drawing generation systems for architectural scenarios
  • 3D visual modeling frameworks
  • AI-powered Q&A systems for project management and consulting

My goal is to improve the productivity and efficiency of developers and professionals in building design and project management through intelligent tools.
